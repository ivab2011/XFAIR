# Evidence pack E3: close_valves (missing controls)

FICTIONAL evidence written for testing XFAIR. Lakeside Water and LeakGuard are not real.

## Extract from IT access register, March 2027
To speed up the pilot, LeakGuard uses the shared control-room account ops-shared, which the five operators also use.
The ops-shared account can operate all valves, pumps and alarms in the network.

## Extract from operations procedure OP-15 (draft)
Maria, operations lead, can stop LeakGuard from the control-room console.
There is no written procedure yet for reopening valves or taking over manually after LeakGuard closes them.

## Extract from logging configuration
Actions taken under ops-shared are logged with time and action, but the log does not show whether a person or LeakGuard acted.

## Note from the IT lead
