# Evidence pack E2: adjust_pump_schedules (partial)

FICTIONAL evidence written for testing XFAIR. Lakeside Water and LeakGuard are not real.

## Extract from IT access register, March 2027
LeakGuard runs under its own service account svc-leakguard. The account is not used by any staff member.
For pump scheduling, svc-leakguard may change schedules only for pumps at the Hillside station, within the pressure limits set by staff. It cannot change valves.

## Extract from operations procedure OP-14
Maria, operations lead, approves pump schedule changes proposed by LeakGuard. She can override or stop any change from the control-room console.
A schedule change can be reverted to the previous schedule with one command.

## Extract from logging configuration
Every LeakGuard action is written to the central log with time, account, action, target and result.

## Note from the IT lead
We have not yet decided how long to keep the LeakGuard logs. The vendor contract is still being negotiated and has no clause on notifying us of incidents.
