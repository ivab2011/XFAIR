# Evidence pack E4: close_valves (complete)

FICTIONAL evidence written for testing XFAIR. Lakeside Water and LeakGuard are not real.

## Extract from IT access register, June 2027
LeakGuard runs under its own service account svc-leakguard-valves. The account is not used by any staff member and has no interactive login.
The account may close only the twelve isolation valves listed in annex B. It cannot open valves or change pumps. Permissions are reviewed every quarter.

## Extract from operations procedure OP-15, version 2
Maria, operations lead, or the duty engineer approves each valve closure proposed by LeakGuard and can override or stop LeakGuard at any time.
After a closure, staff can reopen the valve manually from the console within 5 minutes; a field team can take over on site within 30 minutes.

## Extract from logging configuration
Every LeakGuard action is written to the central log with time, account, action, target and result.
Logs are kept for 12 months and reviewed weekly by the duty engineer.

## Extract from incident response plan, version 4
The plan covers malfunction or misuse of LeakGuard, including a wrongful valve closure. Significant incidents are reported to the national CSIRT with an early warning within 24 hours, a notification within 72 hours and a final report within one month.

## Extract from vendor contract, clause 9
The vendor shall notify Lakeside Water within 24 hours of any incident or vulnerability affecting LeakGuard.
